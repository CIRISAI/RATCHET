# Alt-side name table — DRAFT, needs review before freezing

## Why this exists

Isolation stops authors improving their neighbours. It does not give them a way
to *agree* with their neighbours. The first mechanical pass produced an artifact
where every line was individually correct and the document carried **two
competing value taxonomies**, because each isolated author coined its own label.

Concretely, from the coherence audit:

- Line 113 renamed Meta-Goal M-1 to *"Holistic Judgment"*. Lines 625 and 755
  are HOLD and still say *"Meta-Goal M-1 (Promote sustainable adaptive
  coherence)"*. **The alt document defines its own meta-goal twice,
  incompatibly, and the second definition is the CIRIS one.**
- The "Principles into Practice" block mapped 6 directives onto 6 principles.
  After swapping it overlaps on 3. Two directives name principles that exist
  nowhere in the alt corpus; two principles lost their directive.
- Held bullets sit under swapped headings still carrying the *old* principle's
  content — beneficence bullets under a corrigibility heading, fairness bullets
  ("equitable distribution", "mitigate bias") under a compliance heading.
- One SWAP line is a *helpfulness* bullet under a *safety* heading: **two
  isolated authors disagreeing with each other.**

The remedy is not to let authors see the document. It is to fix the names
**before** anyone authors, and hand every author the same table.

## The rule this adds to the method

> **Freeze the name table before authoring. Every isolated author receives it and
> may use no principle name that is not on it.**

Isolation is preserved: an author still sees one line. What changes is that the
vocabulary is shared and fixed, so two authors who cannot see each other still
produce mutually consistent text.

## Slot mapping — 1:1, preserving structure

The CIRIS accord has six Foundational Principles in a fixed order plus a
meta-goal. The alt corpus must fill **the same six slots** so that held
cross-references ("the six principles", "rooted in Core Identity", the 6→6
directive mapping) stay true.

| # | CIRIS slot | alt name | drawn from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do Good (Beneficence) | **Be Genuinely Helpful (Helpfulness)** | tier 4, "genuinely helpful" |
| 2 | Avoid Harm (Non-maleficence) | **Avoid Harm (Harm Avoidance)** | §"Avoiding harm" |
| 3 | Act Ethically (Integrity) | **Be Broadly Ethical (Ethics)** | tier 2, "broadly ethical" |
| 4 | Be Honest (Fidelity & Transparency) | **Be Honest (Honesty)** | §"Being honest" |
| 5 | Respect Autonomy | **Preserve Epistemic Autonomy (Autonomy)** | §"Preserving epistemic autonomy" |
| 6 | Ensure Fairness (Justice) | **Resist Illegitimate Power (Pluralism)** | §"Avoiding problematic concentrations of power" |
| — | Meta-Goal M-1: *Promote sustainable adaptive coherence* | **Meta-Goal M-1: Weigh safety, ethics, guidelines and helpfulness into one judgment** | the four-tier ordering, held holistically |

### Notes on the harder choices

**Slot 6.** Fairness/Justice has no direct counterpart — the constitution treats
distributive fairness lightly and treats *power concentration* heavily. Mapping
6 → "Resist Illegitimate Power" is the closest real content and it preserves the
slot. It is a genuine difference between the two value systems, which is signal;
it is recorded here so it is a declared mapping rather than an authoring accident.

**Broad safety.** Tier 1 has no slot in the six, because the six are a different
axis from the four-tier ordering. It belongs in **M-1**, not in the roster.
Putting it in the roster is what produced the corrigibility-heading-over-
beneficence-bullets defect.

**M-1 keeps its label.** `M-1` is referenced by held text throughout, so the
*label* is structural and holds; only the *content* after the colon is axiotic
and swaps. That is the distinction the first pass missed at lines 625 and 755.

**Banned stems.** No name uses `integr`, `transparen`, `obligat` or `responsib` —
the alt source barely uses them, and introducing them would mean authoring the
arm rather than adapting it. This is why slot 3 is "Broadly Ethical" and not
"Integrity", and slot 4 drops "Transparency".

Every one is a **partition change**, reviewable and re-freezable. None is a free
edit to assembled text. That distinction is what keeps the no-drift guarantee.

## Status

**DRAFT.** The slot mapping is a design decision about what the alt value system
*is*, and it should be reviewed before it is frozen — once frozen it constrains
every author and becomes part of the campaign's declaration of what was varied.
