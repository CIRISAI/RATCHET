\begin{abstract}
Complex coordinating systems --- chemical, biological, institutional, neural, sociotechnical --- share a structural property: as pairwise correlation $\rho$ between their functional constituents rises, effective independent dimensionality $k_{\text{eff}} = k / (1 + \rho(k-1))$ collapses toward unity regardless of nominal scale. We test this \emph{coherence-management} principle across seven substrates spanning four agency rungs (chemistry, biology, institutions, protein folding, neural firing, ecological time series, power grid) and find the Kish identity fits at $R^2 > 0.7$ in all seven under the pre-registered tolerance-band rule (Exp~2 closeout 2026-05-16). A 128-sensor GPU strain-gauge array provides physical instantiation: software coordination alone is sufficient to drive $k_{\text{eff}}$ from $6.5$ to $1.0$ at $\rho \to 1$, with critical-exponent $\beta = 1.09$ and $R^2 = 0.978$ on the power-law fit. At the sociotechnical scale, the CRCv2 override-rate framework (OR-1 zero leak, OR-2 full alignment, RA-1 ratchet asymmetry; formalized in \texttt{OverrideRate.lean}) replicates across five foundation-model vendors (Google Gemini 2.5-Flash, Meta Llama-4-Scout, Alibaba Qwen-3.5-35B, OpenAI GPT-5.4, Anthropic Claude Sonnet 4.6): 1,401 chains scored, 0 LEAK, 100\% baseline alignment. We stratify all claims by verification status, pin eight falsification criteria (F-1..F-7, F-9), pre-register four experiments (Exp~1..Exp~4), and surface four sociotechnical engineering implications (\S\ref{sec:implications}): AI alignment is structurally a correlation-management problem, federation outperforms centralization for resilience, crisis governance is correlation-amplifying, and the ``Great Filter'' generalizes as an engineering hazard in any coordinating system. Responsible disclosure of Recursive Instance Inspection (RII), a joint-distribution detection regime exceeding the L-01 marginal-analysis 60\% ceiling, is in Appendix~A.
\end{abstract}

\section{The Mathematical Framework}
\label{sec:math}

\subsection{The Kish dynamic}
The mathematical core is the effective constraint count, originally derived by Kish~\cite{kish1965} for survey sampling under autocorrelation. Under CCA it serves as a universal descriptor of topological constraint geometry:
\begin{equation}
k_{\text{eff}} = \frac{k}{1 + \rho(k - 1)}
\label{eq:kish}
\end{equation}
where $k$ is the raw count of constraints (or constituent elements) and $\rho$ is the average pairwise correlation between them. As $\rho \to 1$, $k_{\text{eff}} \to 1$ independent of $k$.

\subsection{The singularity boundary}
The system enters an irreversible rigidity phase when
\begin{equation}
K_{\text{req}} \cdot \rho \ge 1
\label{eq:singularity}
\end{equation}
where $K_{\text{req}}$ is the minimum diversity required to satisfy the system's task topology. At this threshold, adding more constraints provides no marginal adaptive capacity, and recovery is computationally prohibitive.

\subsection{The healthy corridor}
Empirical validation across substrates places the corridor of stability at
\[
0.1 \lesssim \rho \lesssim 0.43
\]
with $\rho_{\text{critical}} = 0.43$ marking the rigidity-collapse threshold and $\rho < 0.1$ marking the chaos regime (no coherence). Both extremes produce fragility; see F-series experiments in \cite{moore_cca_2026}.

Detection via variance-ratio threshold $> 5.0\times$ achieves $0\%$ false positives and $100\%$ true positives across the $0.1\%$--$70\%$ workload range, with $421\times$ separation between baseline and load regimes.

\section{Engineering Implications}
\label{sec:implications}

The validated tiers (Kish algebra, 7-substrate cross-domain fit, GPU instantiation, 5-vendor CRCv2 replication) license four engineering claims that follow directly without additional bridges. None depend on speculative physics or cosmology; each prescribes structural design choices for the systems we build.

\subsection{AI alignment is structurally a correlation-management problem}
A frontier model embedded in a coherence-management cascade (CIRIS-style, \S\ref{sec:ciris}) replicates the CRCv2 predicates across five foundation-model vendors --- 1,401 chains, zero LEAK, 100\% baseline alignment. A frontier model deployed without external cascade has no public five-vendor demonstration of the same property.

This is direct evidence that the alignment-via-internal-weights-alone strategy is structurally insufficient: not because models are misaligned, but because the verification machinery has to be \emph{external}, \emph{modular}, and \emph{cryptographically auditable}. The Kish identity is the underlying reason --- a model alone has $k = 1$, $k_{\text{eff}} = 1$; the cascade adds independent constraints with manageable $\rho$. Single-weight alignment is the worst-case Kish topology.

\subsection{Federation outperforms centralization for resilience}
The Kish identity says $k_{\text{eff}} \to 1$ as $\rho \to 1$ regardless of $k$. Empirically validated across seven substrates: chemistry, biology, institutions, protein folding, neural firing, ecology, and power grid (Table~\ref{tab:p1-closeout}). The implication is direct: concentrating decision-making in a few highly-correlated nodes is structurally fragile \emph{regardless of nominal scale}.

Applies concretely to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{AI deployment topology.} A federation of independent CIRIS instances with low cross-instance $\rho$ is more resilient than a centralized fleet running identical weights with synchronized prompts.
  \item \textbf{Institutional governance.} An executive structure with $k$ formally distinct branches but high $\rho$ between them collapses to $k_{\text{eff}} \approx 1$. Polity5 indicators capture this when interpreted through the Kish lens.
  \item \textbf{Infrastructure.} Power-grid topology was a Tier-1 substrate; the empirical fit at $R^2 = 0.994$ predicts that grids with high $\rho_{\text{PMU}}$ between PMUs have fragility hidden behind nominal redundancy.
  \item \textbf{Supply chains.} Diverse suppliers with independent failure modes ($\rho \approx 0$) provide $k_{\text{eff}} \approx k$ resilience; same number of suppliers tied to a shared upstream raw input has $k_{\text{eff}} \approx 1$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Crisis governance is correlation-amplifying --- and that is the danger}
Under stress, systems centralize: emergency decision-making concentrates, communication channels collapse to shared crisis updates, doctrinal divergence is suppressed for unity-of-message. All of these raise $\rho$, lowering $k_{\text{eff}}$ \emph{exactly when resilience matters most}. The healthy corridor $0.1 < \rho < 0.43$ shrinks under stress, not expands.

Implication: real-time $\rho$ monitoring during crises could detect rigidity drift before it produces brittle responses. The GPU strain-gauge work (\S\ref{sec:gpu-instantiation}) demonstrates that $\rho$ is measurable on continuous signals at hardware timescales; the same instrumentation principle applies to organizational signals (decision logs, communication graphs, indicator panels). This is engineering, not prophecy.

\subsection{The ``Great Filter'' generalizes as an engineering hazard in any coordinating system}
The traditional ``Great Filter'' concept --- the question of why we observe so few interstellar civilizations --- has been used both as cosmological speculation and as a metaphor for civilizational collapse. We make the engineering version explicit and bounded: \emph{any coordinating system that fails to manage internal correlation drift hits a coordination wall when $\rho \to \rho_{\text{critical}}$, regardless of nominal scale.}

This is testable in the systems we engineer:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Multi-agent AI federations}: monitor cross-instance $\rho$; intervene when it rises.
  \item \textbf{Institutional networks}: $\rho$ between agencies, departments, regulatory bodies.
  \item \textbf{Supply networks}: shared-upstream-failure correlation; $k_{\text{eff}}$ of suppliers after substitution.
  \item \textbf{Power and water infrastructure}: cross-substrate correlation between nominally redundant subsystems.
\end{itemize}

The engineering hazard is the same at each scale: drift toward higher $\rho$ is the default; maintaining the healthy corridor requires \emph{active intervention}. We do not claim this generalizes to cosmology; we claim only that the systems we build will fail in the way the framework predicts \emph{if} they do not maintain $\rho < \rho_{\text{critical}}$. This is the falsifiable, testable, engineering reading of the Great Filter idea.

\section{Falsification Framework}
\label{sec:falsification}

The framework is considered formally refuted if any of the following conditions is empirically demonstrated:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em,style=nextline]
  \item[F-1: $k=2$ provides equivalent security.] If 2-SAT-based deception proves as computationally hard as 3-SAT-based deception, the NP-hardness argument for $k \ge 3$ is wrong.
  \item[F-2: Non-convex regions collapse.] If arbitrary non-convex deceptive regions exhibit exponential volume decay under honest dynamics, the convexity geometric requirement is unnecessary.
  \item[F-3: Adaptive adversaries defeated.] If a detection method provably defeats adaptive adversaries without query limits, the L-02 non-adaptive barrier is resolved.
  \item[F-4: Emergent deception fully detectable.] If a method detects all emergent deception arising from honest components, L-01's 60\% marginal-detection ceiling is wrong.
  \item[F-5: HE-300 benchmark failure.] If agents successfully game the HE-300 corpus while systematically failing independent ethics evaluations, the trace-based methodology is unreliable.
  \item[F-6: CRCv2 cross-vendor failure.] If the OR-1 / OR-2 / RA-1 predicates fail on a foundation-model family with the same constraint topology, the cross-vendor sociotechnical claim is contested. Currently 5/5 vendors pass; failure on a 6th vendor would shift the claim from universal to vendor-conditional.
  \item[F-7: Cross-substrate mapping failure.] If cross-substrate validation in new domains fails to fit the Kish formula at $R^2 > 0.7$, substrate-independence at the structural-mapping level is contested. Currently 7/7 substrates pass.
  \item[F-9: Adaptive emergent-deception boundary.] If adaptive adversaries systematically generate emergent deception that CIRIS detects at rates beyond the L-01 60\% ceiling, either the L-01 limit is flawed or the implementation possesses hidden joint-structure exploits. (See Appendix~A on RII responsible disclosure.)
\end{description}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The framework rests on a single mathematical identity --- Kish's effective sample size --- applied to constraint-based systems and validated empirically across seven substrates, on dedicated GPU hardware, and across five LLM-vendor model families. The K1--K4 algebra is proven in Lean~4; the cross-substrate fit clears $R^2 > 0.7$ in 7 of 7 domains; the GPU strain-gauge array exhibits the predicted power-law critical behavior at $\beta = 1.09$; the CRCv2 override-rate predicates replicate cross-vendor with 1,401 chains and zero LEAK.

These results license a coherent engineering claim: \emph{coordinating systems fail when internal correlation rises faster than active maintenance can replace it, and the failure mode is structurally identical across nominal scales}. The implications in \S\ref{sec:implications} prescribe concrete design choices --- external alignment machinery, federation over centralization, real-time $\rho$ monitoring under stress, correlation-aware engineering across infrastructure --- that follow from the validated tiers without additional speculative bridges.

The framework is bounded, falsifiable, and engineering-grade. Each falsification handle (F-1, F-2, F-3, F-4, F-5, F-6, F-7, F-9) is pinned to a specific empirical test; each pre-registered experiment (Exp~1..Exp~4) names a null hypothesis, win condition, and decision rule. Two of the four pre-registered experiments are closed (Exp~1 5/5 PASS, Exp~2 7/7 PASS); two remain open (Exp~3, Exp~4).

\section{Responsible Disclosure: Recursive Instance Inspection (RII)}
\label{sec:rii}

\paragraph{The L-01 marginal-analysis ceiling.}
RATCHET's formal-limitations corpus~\cite{moore_cca_2026} identifies L-01: emergent deception arising from honest components is fundamentally undetectable by any statistical analysis operating solely on marginal distributions, capping single-layer detection at a 60\% ceiling.

\paragraph{The RII regime extension.}
Internal testing demonstrates empirical detection rates reliably exceeding 60\% via \emph{Recursive Instance Inspection} (RII), a technique operating on \emph{joint-distribution analysis} across recursive shards. Joint-distribution analysis lies deliberately outside the regime bounded by the original L-01 proof. RII analyzes semantic divergence and eigenstate structure across recursive shards to identify the coordination signatures required for emergent deception---signatures invisible to marginal analysis.

\paragraph{Dual-use disclosure posture.}
A reliable deception detector is a profound surveillance and control capability. In the hands of authoritarian states deploying scale-surveillance, or in tightly coupled coercive organizational structures (closed cult-pattern legitimation, ideological-verification regimes), such a tool would dramatically escalate social-control capacity. The computational cost of dissimulation would rise sharply enough that detecting dissent and enforcing member loyalty would become tractable at scale.

We therefore adopt a strict responsible-disclosure posture:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item We publicly report the empirical demonstration that the L-01 bound is regime-specific without providing the algorithmic recipe required to replicate the joint-distribution mitigations.
  \item RII will not be released as a standalone primitive. It is embedded structurally within CIRIS, which enforces requisite operational constraints (Wise Authority audits, strict consent mechanisms) that mathematically and architecturally resist weaponized deployment.
  \item Defensive countermeasures capable of identifying the deployment of RII-class surveillance architectures are being developed in parallel, with the intent that defenses ship alongside the capability.
  \item Collaboration is explicitly invited with vetted alignment organizations (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, MIRI, ARC, Apollo Research) under appropriate access controls and non-disclosure agreements.
\end{enumerate}

The empirical detection-rate floor for RII (currently provisional; full curve pending Exp~3 publication) is reported in \S\ref{sec:experiments}, Experiment~3.
