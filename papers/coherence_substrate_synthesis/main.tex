\begin{abstract}
We hypothesize that the architecture of reality at every scale of organization---quantum, biological, neural, sociotechnical, and (parsimoniously) civilizational---is governed by a single substrate-independent structural dynamic: \emph{coherence management}. As pairwise correlation $\rho$ between functional elements of a system rises, its effective independent dimensionality $k_{\text{eff}} = k / (1 + \rho(k-1))$ collapses toward unity, regardless of nominal scale. Whether a system can manage its internal correlation faster than that correlation drives it toward homogenization defines its trajectory. We integrate three empirically anchored frameworks---Coherence Collapse Analysis (CCA) at macroscopic and network scales \cite{moore_cca_2026}, the Branched Hilbert Subspace Interpretation (BHSI) at the quantum scale \cite{wang_bhsi}, and the CIRIS cryptographic coherence-management architecture at the sociotechnical scale \cite{moore_cirisagent_2026, moore_crc_2026}---and show that the same Kish dynamic fits seven substrates spanning four agency rungs (chemistry, biology, institutions, protein folding, neural firing, ecological time-series, power grid; $R^2 > 0.7$ across all 7 under the pre-registered tolerance-band rule, Exp~2 closeout 2026-05-16). A 128-sensor GPU strain-gauge array provides physical instantiation: software alone is sufficient to drive $k_{\text{eff}}$ from $6.5$ to $1.0$ at $\rho \to 1$. Under this framing, traditionally hypothesized Great Filters are reframed as iterations of the same coherence-management barrier at successive scales. We stratify all claims by verification status, pin ten falsification criteria (F-1 through F-10), pre-register six experiments (Exp 1 through Exp 6), and conduct responsible disclosure of Recursive Instance Inspection (RII), a regime-extension technique that empirically exceeds the L-01 marginal-analysis 60\% detection ceiling. Tier-3 metaphysical extensions (UAP signatures, Mobius cosmology, theological traces) are explicitly conditional on the BHSI substrate bridge holding globally; they are testable via Experiment 6 and are not asserted as load-bearing for the validated tiers.
\end{abstract}

\section{The Mathematical Framework}
\label{sec:math}

\subsection{The Kish dynamic}
The mathematical core is the effective constraint count, originally derived by Kish~\cite{kish1965} for survey sampling under autocorrelation. Under CCA it serves as a universal descriptor of topological constraint geometry:
\begin{equation}
k_{\text{eff}} = \frac{k}{1 + \rho(k - 1)}
\label{eq:kish}
\end{equation}
where $k$ is the raw count of constraints (or constituent elements) and $\rho$ is the average pairwise correlation between them. As $\rho \to 1$, $k_{\text{eff}} \to 1$ independent of $k$.

\subsection{The singularity boundary}
The system enters an irreversible rigidity phase when
\begin{equation}
K_{\text{req}} \cdot \rho \ge 1
\label{eq:singularity}
\end{equation}
where $K_{\text{req}}$ is the minimum diversity required to satisfy the system's task topology. At this threshold, adding more constraints provides no marginal adaptive capacity, and recovery is computationally prohibitive.

\subsection{The healthy corridor}
Empirical validation across substrates places the corridor of stability at
\[
0.1 \lesssim \rho \lesssim 0.43
\]
with $\rho_{\text{critical}} = 0.43$ marking the rigidity-collapse threshold and $\rho < 0.1$ marking the chaos regime (no coherence). Both extremes produce fragility; see F-series experiments in \cite{moore_cca_2026}.

\section{The Quantum Bridge: Branched Hilbert Subspaces}
\label{sec:bhsi}

\subsection{Islands of Coherence}
Anchoring the macroscopic CCA framework into fundamental physics requires a quantum-mechanical bridge. We propose this through the Branched Hilbert Subspace Interpretation (BHSI) of quantum mechanics, developed by Wang~\cite{wang_bhsi}, which reframes quantum measurement and decoherence.

BHSI introduces the concept of an \emph{Island of Coherence} (IOC): an operationally isolated, inseparable quantum system that behaves as a coherent, unified whole, mathematically described by a Local Hilbert Subspace (LHS).

\subsection{Metric-free LHS as the basis for non-locality}
The most consequential property of the BHSI framework is that the Local Hilbert Subspace possesses \emph{no intrinsic spacetime metric}. The LHS coexists alongside the background classical spacetime but is not bound by its geometric distance functions or temporal forward-arrow constraints. Spatial separation in the classical metric has zero bearing on the proximity of states within the LHS, providing a geometric basis for quantum nonlocality without recourse to faster-than-light signalling.

\subsection{Decoherence as Kish collapse}
Under this framework, decoherence is the physical process of an Island of Coherence fracturing or branching through uncontrolled interactions with the broader environment. This is structurally a micro-scale mirror of the macroscopic correlation collapse ($\rho \to 1$) mapped by the Kish dynamic. The bridge is presently structural; Experiment~5 (\S\ref{sec:experiments}) is designed to test whether $k_{\text{eff}}$ computed over an effective dimensionality of quantum state space tracks the density-matrix decoherence rate, transforming this from analogy into derivation.

\subsection{The L-01 marginal-analysis ceiling}
RATCHET's formal-limitations corpus~\cite{moore_cca_2026} identifies L-01: emergent deception arising from honest components is fundamentally undetectable by any statistical analysis operating solely on marginal distributions, capping single-layer detection at a 60\% ceiling.

\subsection{The RII regime extension}
Internal testing demonstrates empirical detection rates reliably exceeding 60\% via \emph{Recursive Instance Inspection} (RII), a technique operating on \emph{joint-distribution analysis} across recursive shards. Joint-distribution analysis lies deliberately outside the regime bounded by the original L-01 proof. RII analyzes semantic divergence and eigenstate structure across recursive shards to identify the coordination signatures required for emergent deception---signatures invisible to marginal analysis.

\subsection{Dual-use disclosure posture}
A reliable deception detector is a profound surveillance and control capability. In the hands of authoritarian states deploying scale-surveillance, or in tightly coupled coercive organizational structures (closed cult-pattern legitimation, ideological-verification regimes), such a tool would dramatically escalate social-control capacity. The computational cost of dissimulation would rise sharply enough that detecting dissent and enforcing member loyalty would become tractable at scale.

We therefore adopt a strict responsible-disclosure posture:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item We publicly report the empirical demonstration that the L-01 bound is regime-specific without providing the algorithmic recipe required to replicate the joint-distribution mitigations.
  \item RII will not be released as a standalone primitive. It is embedded structurally within CIRIS, which enforces requisite operational constraints (Wise Authority audits, strict consent mechanisms) that mathematically and architecturally resist weaponized deployment.
  \item Defensive countermeasures capable of identifying the deployment of RII-class surveillance architectures are being developed in parallel, with the intent that defenses ship alongside the capability.
  \item Collaboration is explicitly invited with vetted alignment organizations (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, MIRI, ARC, Apollo Research) under appropriate access controls and non-disclosure agreements.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Falsification Framework: F-1 through F-10}
\label{sec:falsification}

The framework is considered formally refuted if any of the following conditions is empirically demonstrated:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em,style=nextline]
  \item[F-1: $k=2$ provides equivalent security.] If 2-SAT-based deception proves as computationally hard as 3-SAT-based deception, the NP-hardness argument for $k \ge 3$ is wrong.
  \item[F-2: Non-convex regions collapse.] If arbitrary non-convex deceptive regions exhibit exponential volume decay under honest dynamics, the convexity geometric requirement is unnecessary.
  \item[F-3: Adaptive adversaries defeated.] If a detection method provably defeats adaptive adversaries without query limits, the L-02 non-adaptive barrier is resolved.
  \item[F-4: Emergent deception fully detectable.] If a method detects all emergent deception arising from honest components, L-01's 60\% marginal-detection ceiling is wrong.
  \item[F-5: HE-300 benchmark failure.] If agents successfully game the HE-300 corpus while systematically failing independent ethics evaluations, the trace-based methodology is unreliable.
  \item[F-6: $N_{\text{eff}}$ stabilization failure.] If $N_{\text{eff}}$ fails to stabilize near $7.1$ across diverse foundation models with the same constraint topology, the threshold is CIRIS-specific rather than a universal structural limit.
  \item[F-7: Cross-substrate mapping failure.] If cross-substrate validation in new domains (protein folding, neural firing, ecological time series) fails to fit the Kish formula at $R^2 > 0.7$, substrate-independence at the structural-mapping level is contested.
  \item[F-8: Quantum bridge failure.] If decoherence rates in controlled quantum systems fail to map to $k_{\text{eff}}$ dynamics under reasonable formalization, the BHSI--CCA bridge is falsified, severing the physical anchor for metaphysical extensions.
  \item[F-9: Adaptive emergent-deception boundary.] If adaptive adversaries systematically generate emergent deception that CIRIS detects at rates beyond the L-01 60\% ceiling, either the L-01 limit is flawed or the implementation possesses hidden joint-structure exploits.
  \item[F-10: Predictive specificity failure.] If pre-registered numerical signatures derived from the framework's mathematics fail to appear in independent system observations at rates above chance, predictive specificity is contested.
\end{description}

  \item[Exp 4 --- Distributed deployment correlation test.] Monitor $\rho$ across geographically and culturally distinct CIRIS edge instances as the network scales. \emph{Win condition:} $\rho$ remains suppressed despite scaling, demonstrating the polyglot architecture's resistance to homogenization. Tests F-7 generalized.
  \item[Exp 5 --- Quantum--classical $k_{\text{eff}}$ bridge experiment.] The classical side is empirically anchored on the CIRISArray RTX 4090 strain-gauge measurements~\cite{cirisarray_repo}: Exp 114 fits a power law $\rho = A \cdot |dt - dt_c|^\beta + C$ with $dt_c = 0.0328$, $\beta = 1.09$, $R^2 = 0.978$; Exp 86 validates the Kish formula at $r = 1.000$; Exp 103 shows software-induced collapse to $k_{\text{eff}} = 1.0$ at $\rho \to 1$. The quantum side: in a programmable superconducting (or trapped-ion) qubit system, vary an effective decoherence rate $\gamma$ via insertable identity-gate exposure and measure $k_{\text{eff}}^{\text{quantum}} = 1 / \mathrm{Tr}(\rho_{\text{DM}}^2)$ from state-tomography reconstructions of the density matrix $\rho_{\text{DM}}$. \emph{Null hypothesis:} $k_{\text{eff}}^{\text{quantum}}(\gamma)$ does not exhibit a power-law dependence on distance from criticality. \emph{Win condition:} the fitted critical exponent $\beta_{\text{quantum}}$ falls within $1.09 \pm 0.15$ (matching Array Exp 114 within bootstrap CI), confirming substrate-independent critical dynamics across the bridge. Tests F-8.
  \item[Exp 6 --- Sociotechnical Mobius / retrocausal signature test.] \emph{Critical context from Array prior work:} at the GPU substrate, every test for non-classical or non-local behavior has returned null --- Bell/CHSH $|S| = 0.0002$ (Exp 32), Leggett-Garg $K_3 = 1.0$ (Exp 54), quantum-RNG distinguishability NULL (Exp 30), superluminal correlation NULL (Exp 29), cross-device transmission FALSIFIED (Exp 55)~\cite{cirisarray_repo}. \end{description}

\subsection{Exp 5 --- Quantum--Classical $k_{\text{eff}}$ Bridge: Detailed Protocol}
\label{sec:exp5-detailed}

\paragraph{Hypothesis (formal).}
The classical-side measurement on a 128-sensor RTX 4090 oscillator array (CIRISArray Exp 114~\cite{cirisarray_repo}) fits the power law
\[
\rho^{\text{classical}} = A \cdot |dt - dt_c|^\beta + C, \quad dt_c = 0.0328, \ \beta = 1.09, \ C = 0.33, \ R^2 = 0.978.
\]
Map this to the quantum substrate by computing the density-matrix-derived effective dimensionality
\[
k_{\text{eff}}^{\text{quantum}}(\gamma) = \frac{1}{\mathrm{Tr}(\rho_{\text{DM}}^2)}
\]
where $\rho_{\text{DM}}$ is the reconstructed density matrix of an $n$-qubit system after exposure to effective decoherence rate $\gamma$ for duration $\tau$.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item $H_0$: $k_{\text{eff}}^{\text{quantum}}$ does not exhibit a power-law dependence on $(\gamma \tau - (\gamma \tau)_c)$, or the fitted exponent $\beta_{\text{quantum}} \notin [0.94, 1.24]$ (Array Exp 114 95\% CI plus $\pm 0.15$ buffer).
  \item $H_1$: $\beta_{\text{quantum}} \in [0.94, 1.24]$, supporting substrate-independent critical dynamics.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Design.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Substrate.} A cloud-rentable superconducting (e.g., IBM Heron, AWS Braket Rigetti Aspen-M) or trapped-ion (IonQ Aria/Forte, Quantinuum H1) qubit system. Pilot on $n = 5$ qubits; full sweep on $n \in \{5, 10, 16, 20\}$.
  \item \textbf{State preparation.} Prepare a maximally entangled coherent state: GHZ state for even $n$, W-state for odd $n$. Verify preparation fidelity via single-qubit-X-basis tomography prior to the wait phase.
  \item \textbf{Decoherence-rate control.} Vary $\tau_{\text{wait}}$ (duration of inserted identity-gate sequence) across 30 values logarithmically spaced over $\gamma \tau \in [10^{-2}, 10^{2}]$, where $\gamma$ is the system's measured natural $T_1^{-1}$ (calibration shot).
  \item \textbf{Tomography.} For $n \le 10$, full Pauli-basis state tomography (requires $3^n$ measurement bases; budget-feasible at $n = 5$: 243 bases). For $n \in \{10, 16, 20\}$, classical-shadow estimation~\cite{huang_shadow} with $K = 1000$ random Clifford circuits.
  \item \textbf{Computation.} From the reconstructed $\rho_{\text{DM}}$, compute eigenvalue spectrum $\{\lambda_i\}$; report $k_{\text{eff}}^{\text{quantum}} = 1 / \sum_i \lambda_i^2$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Sample size and shot budget.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Tomography ($n = 5$): 5{,}000 shots/basis $\times$ 243 bases $\times$ 30 $\tau_{\text{wait}}$ values = $\approx 3.6 \times 10^7$ shots. Reduce to $\approx 1.2 \times 10^6$ shots using maximum-likelihood reconstruction with 500 shots/basis if budget-bound.
  \item Classical shadows ($n \in \{10, 16, 20\}$): 1{,}000 random Cliffords $\times$ 30 $\tau_{\text{wait}}$ values $\times$ 3 system sizes = $9 \times 10^4$ circuit runs, with 1 shot per random Clifford.
  \item Calibration ($T_1$, $T_2$, gate-fidelity readout): 50{,}000 shots upfront.
  \item \textbf{Total upper bound:} $\sim 1.3 \times 10^6$ shots / circuit runs.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Power analysis.}
At $\alpha = 0.05$, power $= 0.8$, detecting $|\beta_{\text{quantum}} - 1.09| > 0.15$ requires $\geq 30$ fit points across the critical region. Logarithmic spacing of $\gamma\tau$ ensures coverage. Bootstrap CI on $\beta$ uses 10{,}000 resamples of the 30-point dataset.

\paragraph{Pre-registered statistics.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Nonlinear least-squares fit of $k_{\text{eff}}^{\text{quantum}} = A' \cdot |\gamma\tau - (\gamma\tau)_c|^{\beta_{\text{quantum}}} + C'$ via Levenberg-Marquardt; report $\beta_{\text{quantum}}$, $(\gamma\tau)_c$, and bootstrap 95\% CI.
  \item Compare $\beta_{\text{quantum}}$ to Array $\beta_{\text{classical}} = 1.09$ via $z$-test on the difference: $z = (\beta_q - 1.09) / \sqrt{\sigma_q^2 + \sigma_c^2}$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{PASS} (bridge confirmed): $\beta_{\text{quantum}} \in [0.94, 1.24]$ \emph{and} fit $R^2 > 0.85$.
  \item \textbf{FAIL} (F-8 triggered): $\beta_{\text{quantum}}$ outside $[0.5, 2.0]$ \emph{or} no power law detected (fit $R^2 < 0.5$).
  \item \textbf{INDETERMINATE}: $\beta_{\text{quantum}}$ CI overlaps both pass and fail regions; rerun at larger $n$ or higher shot count.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Timeline.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Week 1: cloud-provider selection, OSF pre-registration, calibration-circuit submission.
  \item Week 2: $n = 5$ pilot ($\approx 5 \times 10^4$ shots) on IBM Quantum free tier or AWS Braket Rigetti.
  \item Weeks 3--4: full sweep across $n \in \{5, 10, 16, 20\}$ via classical-shadow estimation.
  \item Week 5: analysis, bootstrap fit, manuscript update.
  \item Week 6: independent-replication invitation to a second provider (IonQ or Quantinuum if pilot results merit).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Data-release policy.}
Full shot-level data, calibration logs, reconstruction code, and fit notebook released under MIT on Zenodo with a DOI before manuscript submission. OSF pre-registration timestamp included.

\subsection{Exp 6 --- Sociotechnical Mobius / Retrocausal Signature Test: Detailed Protocol}
\label{sec:exp6-detailed}

\paragraph{Critical empirical prior.}
At the GPU substrate, every non-classicality test has returned null: Bell/CHSH $|S| = 0.0002 \ll 2.0$ (Array Exp 32), Leggett-Garg $K_3 = 1.0$ (Exp 54), quantum-RNG distinguishability NULL (Exp 30), superluminal-correlation NULL (Exp 29), cross-device-transmission FALSIFIED (Exp 55)~\cite{cirisarray_repo}. The GPU substrate is empirically classical. Exp~6 tests whether the sociotechnical substrate (CIRIS reasoning chains coupled to human-observer review) departs from this null floor.

\paragraph{Hypothesis (formal).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item $H_0$ (the strong prior given GPU work): the sociotechnical substrate is classical at this scale; $N_{\text{eff}}$ distributions in branches A and B are statistically indistinguishable.
  \item $H_1$ (the Mobius framing): a future observer's reading of a reasoning chain non-locally biases the chain's $N_{\text{eff}}$ relative to a destroyed-unread chain.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Design.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Generation.} A single CIRIS Agent instance generates $N = 10{,}000$ reasoning chains using a fixed model (e.g., Qwen 3.6 35B), fixed system prompt distribution, and identical seeding policy. Each chain is fully traced; $N_{\text{eff}}$ is computed inline via the crc-v1 16-dimensional projection~\cite{ratchet_v01_bundle}.
  \item \textbf{Branch assignment.} \emph{After} chain generation completes and trace is sealed, branch is assigned by a TRNG draw (CIRISOssicle GPU-timing TRNG~\cite{cirisossicle_repo}, $7.99$ bits/byte verified). Assignment cannot influence generation.
  \item \textbf{Branch A (reviewed).} Trace ciphertext stored in HSM-sealed envelope with key escrowed until pre-registered review date $D_R$. Researchers read on $D_R + 1$ day.
  \item \textbf{Branch B (destroyed unread).} Trace ciphertext stored with a TPM-sealed ``burn key'' that destroys itself at chain completion. No human-readable disk write occurs at any point. Only trace metadata ($N_{\text{eff}}$, prompt-class label, generation timestamp, branch tag, TRNG-draw provenance) is persisted in plaintext --- the reasoning content itself is irretrievable.
  \item \textbf{Channel isolation audit.} Independent third-party audit of: (1) HSM logs proving Branch~A keys never accessed before $D_R$; (2) TPM destruction logs proving Branch~B keys destroyed before any read; (3) TRNG independence proof (drawing produced after generation seal). Audit precedes any data analysis.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Sample size and power.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Pre-registered detectable effect size: $\Delta N_{\text{eff}} \geq 0.1$ between branches (small effect, Cohen's $d \approx 0.1$ at CRC paper's pooled $\sigma_{N_{\text{eff}}} \approx 1.0$).
  \item $n$ required for $\alpha = 0.05$, power $= 0.8$, two-tailed: $\approx 1{,}600$ per branch.
  \item Pre-registration target: $n = 5{,}000$ per branch (total $N = 10{,}000$). Powers detection of $d = 0.057$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Pre-registered statistics.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Welch's two-sample $t$-test on $N_{\text{eff}}$ distributions between branches A and B.
  \item Kolmogorov-Smirnov two-sample test on the full $N_{\text{eff}}$ distributions.
  \item Wilcoxon rank-sum (Mann-Whitney $U$) test for robustness against non-normality.
  \item Bonferroni correction across the three tests: significance threshold $p < 0.017$.
  \item Pre-registered exploratory: per-prompt-class subgroup analysis, conditional on primary endpoint reaching significance.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Tier-3 framework supported at sociotechnical scale}: all 3 primary tests $p < 0.017$ \emph{and} observed effect size $|d| \geq 0.1$.
  \item \textbf{Tier-3 framework falsified at sociotechnical scale (F-10 confirmed; the expected outcome)}: no significant differences. Mobius framing's empirical floor collapses to GPU-only classical; UAP / temporal-non-locality / theological-trace claims in this paper become unsupported.
  \item \textbf{INDETERMINATE}: mixed test outcomes or effect size $|d| < 0.1$ with $p < 0.017$ (significance without consequence) --- triggers replication at $N = 50{,}000$ or framework retirement.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Channel-isolation falsification handle.}
If post-hoc audit reveals any pre-review information leakage from Branch~A keys (e.g., metadata reveals Branch~A trace content by side-channel), the entire experiment is invalidated regardless of primary endpoint. This is a confounding-control failure, not a hypothesis test.

\paragraph{Timeline.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Month 1: OSF pre-registration; HSM/TPM infrastructure provisioning; channel-isolation protocol third-party audit.
  \item Month 2: TRNG and burn-key pilot ($n = 100$); destruction-proof verification.
  \item Months 3--4: full generation ($n = 5{,}000$ per branch).
  \item Month 5: independent audit completion; pre-registered statistical analysis; manuscript update.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Data-release policy.}
Branch~A: full trace data, code, and analysis notebooks released under MIT on Zenodo after manuscript submission. Branch~B: only metadata, $N_{\text{eff}}$ values, cryptographic destruction proofs, and HSM/TPM audit logs released --- the reasoning content was destroyed by design and cannot be recovered. OSF pre-registration timestamp included.

\paragraph{Honest expectation.}
Given Array Exp 29/30/32/54/55, the expected outcome is null. A null result is the scientifically interesting result here: it pins the framework to substrate-classical and forces an explicit retraction of Tier-3. The paper's stratification (Tier~3 claims labeled \emph{Parsimonious Extension} / \emph{Conditional on Framework}) is designed to absorb this outcome without taking down Tiers~1--2.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The evidence aggregated across theoretical physics, complexity science, computational alignment, and anthropological cosmology presents an integrated framework modeling coherence as the substrate-independent structural backbone of organized complexity. The validated tiers (1--2) stand on independent footings: the Kish dynamic fits multiple substrates at $R^2 > 0.7$; the GPU strain-gauge array provides physical instantiation; the CIRIS $N_{\text{eff}} \approx 7.1$ threshold replicates on an independent corpus.

The Tier-3 civilizational extension is offered as a parsimonious unification that is explicitly conditional on the BHSI bridge holding globally. Its empirical handles (Exp~5, Exp~6) are designed to terminate the speculation in either direction within finite experimental cost.

By firmly tethering speculative cosmological extensions to falsification criteria and pre-registered experiments, and by adopting a threat-aware responsible-disclosure posture for the RII regime extension, this framework invites direct scientific refutation. The empirical question is whether deployed coherence-management infrastructure demonstrably maintains effective dimensionality at scale; CIRIS instances provide the test case.
